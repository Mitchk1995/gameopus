Mitchell approved `ashford-lakeside-proposal-v1.png`: “yeah that actually looks pretty decent i like how open the landscape is too”. This approves the architectural and landscape direction. The image is concept art, not a screenshot, and does not establish approval of the existing town or of a finished replacement village.

The new brief is a spread-out lakeside village with gardens, workshops, footpaths, a lively central green, and long open views. Homes should have residents, routines, conversations and occasional small jobs. Keep the cozy, fairly bright direction with convincing surfaces and light.

The next reviewable increment is one complete furnished home for Rowan and Elin. Judge its proportions, materials, doorway, interior camera, household and surrounding breathing room in the actual game before repeating its architecture across the village. Do not call the remaining old town redesigned. Do not treat silence as approval of later artwork.

The first adventure should balance village life, exploration, fighting and a useful reward, starting with gathering, making and meeting people before danger. Preserve existing character progress and saves.

## Concept generation brief

One coherent original, cozy, fairly bright lakeside village; low fieldstone and plaster cottages with slate roofs, coherent joinery and believable human scale; a generous central green, roughly eighteen homes with gardens and gaps, workshops and footpaths, a real shoreline and long open landscape views rather than an enclosing mountain wall. Include readable cottage, inn and smithy studies. No interface, no claim of gameplay. The delivered concept is the image beside this document.
