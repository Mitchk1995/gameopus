# Your first lakeside visit

Preparation note: development is paused at the owner's request. The existing packaged build
passed launch/save checks but still has the old hooded character. The simple base-clothes and
visible-equipment update is saved in source and needs its final review/repackage before these
equipment instructions describe the shipped build.

Open `release\Aldermere\Aldermere.exe` and enter the world. Your existing character and belongings are kept.

1. Follow Lake Street to the water, then take the small path west of the jetty to Rowan and Elin's stone cottage. A new character's journal points you there.
2. Meet both residents, open the door and explore their home. Rowan has a small job if you offer to help.
3. Gather three ordinary logs with the axe in your pack and bring them back. The reward gives you food and a little spending money.
4. Visit Tam at the jetty, try fishing, and cook your catch at the cottage hearth. Follow the next lead when you feel ready to explore.
5. For a quick equipment try, Rowan's 25 coins cover a bronze med helm at Maren's store. Equip it, remove it, and check that your ordinary clothes and hair return. Quit/reopen whenever you want to check your progress was saved.

WASD moves; the mouse looks around; E interacts. I or Tab opens your pack, L opens worn equipment, J opens your journal, and Esc pauses/releases the mouse. The pause screen has the rest of the controls.

This build introduces the first household and fixes around combat, equipment, inventory and saving. The larger village and open landscape are still being developed. The most useful feedback is what felt confusing, cramped, awkward or empty while you were actually playing.
