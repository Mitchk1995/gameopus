# Rowan and Elin's cottage

Approved visual direction: `docs/design/ashford-lakeside-proposal-v1.png`, bottom-left cottage.

Construction reference inspected: [Flint and slate house in Barham](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flint_and_slate_house_in_Barham_-_geograph.org.uk_-_1778122.jpg), Wikimedia / [Geograph photograph 1778122](https://www.geograph.org.uk/photo/1778122) by [David Anstiss](https://www.geograph.org.uk/profile/29880), licensed [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/). The unchanged reference photograph is saved in `references/barham-flint-and-slate.jpg`, with exact Wikimedia metadata in `references/barham-source.json`; it is not shipped in-game or used as a texture. Recognisable construction features: small overlapping slate courses, restrained eaves, solid masonry chimney, deep window reveals, masonry lintels, a modest centred entrance. The game cottage uses one accessible floor rather than copying the photograph's two-storey arrangement.

Additional construction description: [Porch Cottage, Little Rissington](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1340867): coursed local limestone, stone slate and dressed corner stones. The asset uses a pale lime-pointed stone texture and subtle dressed corner blocks rather than oversized exposed cobbles.

Our cottage has an 8 by 7 metre ground floor, a centred entry and clear central passage, a working hearth on the left, dining and mending table on the right, pantry at the back left and bed at the back right. The timber porch shelters the door; Rowan's net basket sits alongside it. Scale is deliberately checked in metres.

Textures from Poly Haven (CC0, verified at https://polyhaven.com/license): plaster_stone_wall_02, old_stone_wall_02, oak_wood_planks, white_plaster_rough_01, roof_slates_03. Existing project assets add rough_linen. The initially tried roof_slates_02 material was replaced after reviewing the first render: its long thin pattern read as wooden shingles. The final roof uses slate03 at a measured seven-metre texture repeat, tinted grey-blue; interior limewash is lightened to maintained warm cream while retaining subdued scanned texture. Material URLs and source metadata are saved beside the editable file.

Garden reference inspected: [Kale and Cabbage in Raised Garden Beds](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kale_and_Cabbage_in_Raised_Garden_Beds_(49200262267).jpg), Alabama Extension, CC0. The unchanged photograph and metadata live in `references/kitchen-garden.jpg` and `references/kitchen-garden-source.json`; it is reference only. The plants are low rosettes with broad cupped outer leaves, overlapping lighter hearts, visible pale midribs, and human-scale planting distances. The game bed uses original cabbage leaf meshes, six [Poly Haven nettle scans](https://polyhaven.com/a/nettle_plant) (CC0, optimised and scaled as young edible greens), and the existing CC0 farm_soil maps. Cabbage surfaces reuse a small section of the nettle scan's normal map for leaf microtexture. Their silhouette and visible veins are modelled individually, not a primitive ball standing in for vegetables.
